Building Excellence. Engineering Innovation. Inspiring Leadership.

For more than twenty-five years, Whitley Enterprise LLC has delivered professional services across construction, engineering, technology, audio-visual integration, leadership development, and ministry. Founded in 1999, the company reflects Dr. Timothy Whitley's commitment to excellence, precision, integrity, and lifelong learning.

With experience spanning military service, commercial construction, electrical engineering, audio production, higher education, and executive leadership, Whitley Enterprise LLC provides comprehensive consulting, design, implementation, and training services throughout the United States.
